The belt goes west. 🏆

UCLA 79, South Carolina 51. The Bruins claim the belt for the 4th time, ending South Carolina's 7th reign after just 2 days since they took it on Apr 3.

The Gamecocks never had a chance. UCLA led 21–10 after one, 36–23 at the half, and put up 25 in the third to blow it open. Gabriela Jaquez led the way with 21 points and 5 assists, Lauren Betts pulled down 11 boards, and the Bruins cruised to a 28-point rout. Tessa Johnson had 14 for South Carolina, Joyce Edwards added 11 rebounds and 3 assists, but the Gamecocks couldn't keep pace.

Belt game No. 1,419 is in the books. UCLA gets it back after last holding it in 2025.

Next up: the Bruins carry the belt home to Los Angeles. vs Lehigh, Mon Nov 2, 12 AM ET. Beat the holder, take the belt.
